Read before you go

These are real places and several of them can kill you. Underground works collapse, flood, and hold air that will not keep you conscious. Ground around WWI and WWII positions still carries unexploded ordnance.

Many sites sit on private or restricted land. An entry in this archive is a record that something exists — it is not permission to enter it.

Check the hazard stamps on a site before you travel, tell someone where you are going, and do not go underground alone.
