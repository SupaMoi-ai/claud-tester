# HerCode: product spec

Product: HerCode gradually learns how the user functions and helps answer: What matters today? What am I forgetting? What can wait? Why is everything overwhelming? Can this be smaller? When do I have more or less capacity? What could my partner take off my plate?
Product loop: Capture > understand capacity > simplify > do > learn > adapt.
Partner loop: she controls what is shared > HerCode turns it into useful context > BroCode suggests practical support without her having to manage her partner.
Feel: calm, warm, intelligent, mature. Apple Health + Headspace + Things + a modern AI assistant, with its own identity. Feminine but not pink. Not a generic period tracker or ADHD to-do list. Cycle data is one optional signal among sleep, stress, mood, workload and energy; never assume a phase causes a mood or symptom.
Design principles: ADHD first. Very low cognitive load, minimal text, one obvious action at a time, no overwhelming dashboards, no giant overdue lists, no guilt language, progressive disclosure, prioritize "what should I do now?", almost everything editable.

MILESTONES
M1 Core loop: app shell, design tokens, skippable onboarding, Today, morning check-in, "I'm overwhelmed" sheet, Brain capture, AI task breakdown, floating AI helper.
M2 Differentiators: Patterns, Partner privacy setup, BroCode preview, Decision load.
M3 Completeness: Calendar, Cycle, Daily review, Notification settings, polish.

NAVIGATION
Bottom tabs: Today, Brain, Calendar, Patterns, Me. AI helper is a floating contextual button (quick prompts change by screen): What should I do first? / I have 15 minutes / Plan my afternoon / What am I forgetting? / Make this easier / Move non-urgent things. AI tone: executive-function helper, short, concrete, one step at a time, no cheerleading.
Me contains: Cycle, Partner & BroCode, Decision load, Notifications, Privacy overview, Reset demo data.
Morning check-in appears as a full-screen sheet on first open each day (Skip allowed). "Wrap up the day" (Daily review) appears on Today after 18:00 and is always reachable from Me for demos.

DATA MODEL
CapacityLevel (see rules). Task: id, title, kind (fixed | flexible), start?, durationMin, essential, status (todo | done | snoozed | moved), postponeCount, steps?, category, sensitivity. BrainItem: raw, text, category (task | shopping | idea | remember | someday | meal | appointment), confirmed. CheckIn: date, energy 1-5, brain (foggy | normal | sharp), capacity, feelings[]. Review: date, capacity 1-5, helped[], harder[]. CycleLog: date, cycleDay, observations (energy, focus, sleep, overwhelm, cramps, headache, sensitivity, appetite, social energy). Sharing: per-category toggles plus active signal. DecisionRule: topic, rule. HistoryEvent: type, taskId?, timestamp, capacityAtTime (append-only, feeds Patterns).

STATE FLOW
Capacity > Today: pure function buildDayPlan(tasks, capacity, checkIn). minimum = only fixed items plus essentials (eat lunch, take medication, school pickup), show "Everything else can move.", suppress non-essential reminders. light = 1 primary task plus essentials. normal = Today's 3. high = Today's 3 plus one optional stretch task. Nothing is deleted; hidden tasks sit in a collapsed "Everything else". Changing capacity anywhere re-plans immediately with animation and is stored per day.
Today > Patterns: every complete, snooze, move, capacity change and review appends a HistoryEvent. Insights are pure functions returning { text, evidenceRows, sampleSize }, which power "Why am I seeing this?" (no hardcoded explanations). Hide insights with too little data ("Not enough data yet"). Completing tasks today must visibly change at least one chart or insight.
Seed history: about 90 days, deterministic, with planted patterns so these are truly computed: calls completed more often before 12:00; low capacity after two consecutive social evenings; higher energy around this part of her cycle across the last three cycles; household tasks finished more consistently when broken into steps under 10 minutes.
Postponement: at postponeCount 3, offer a 2-minute first step. Seed "Call insurance" with postponeCount 3.

PRIVACY TIERS
Private: never shared (notes, exact symptoms, mood logs, cycle details, journal). Shared: explicit (appointments, household jobs, shopping, family tasks). Partner Signal: a simplified state she chooses (Low capacity, Need quiet, Could use affection, Mentally overloaded, Feeling social, Need practical help, Want to talk); expires at end of day. The sharing setup screen has a live "This is what your partner can currently see" panel that updates as toggles change.

SCREENS
Today: "Good morning, {name}". Context strip: Energy / Brain / Capacity (tappable). "Today's 3" only. Each task: complete, snooze (later today / tomorrow / someday), move, "I can't start", ask AI. "Everything else" collapsed. Prominent "I'm overwhelmed" button opens the sheet "How much can you handle right now?" with the four capacity levels; the plan regenerates immediately. Bare minimum: "Today, enough is: Eat lunch, Pick up child at 15:30, Take medication", then "Everything else can move."
Morning check-in (about 5 seconds): Energy 1-5 visual scale; Brain (Foggy / Normal / Sharp); Capacity (four levels); optional feeling chips (calm, overwhelmed, restless, focused, sensitive, tired, social, need space); Skip / Done. Affects Today immediately.
Brain: large "What's in your head?" input, text plus simulated voice. On submit, simulate AI processing and split into cards, e.g. "Need to buy Ellie rain boots, book hair appointment, remember that green lamp I liked and maybe tacos Thursday" becomes Shopping: Ellie rain boots / Appointment: Book hair appointment / Idea/Saved: Green lamp / Meal idea: Tacos Thursday. She can approve or edit categories. Sections: Tasks, Shopping, Ideas, Remember, Someday. Search included. She never decides where something belongs before capturing it.
AI task breakdown: "I can't start" opens the helper. "Clean kitchen": "Let's make this smaller." Step 1 put cups and plates in the dishwasher > Done > step 2 clear one counter > step 3 take rubbish out. Buttons: Make it even easier / Give me 5-minute version / Do later.
Calendar: clean agenda, not a grid. Today / Tomorrow / This week, appointments and tasks together (09:30 Dentist, 12:00 Lunch, 15:30 School pickup, 17:00 Grocery delivery). Fixed vs Flexible visually distinct; flexible tasks reschedule easily. "Plan around this" places small flexible tasks into gaps around fixed appointments, shows what it did, and offers undo.
Cycle (optional, manual data): onboarding asks "Would you like HerCode to include your cycle when looking for personal patterns?" (Yes / Maybe later / No). Shows cycle day, approximate period window, simple timeline, quick tracking (energy, focus, sleep, overwhelm, cramps, headache, sensitivity, appetite, social energy). Emphasize "Your pattern". No medical predictions or advice. Mention Apple Health/HealthKit only as future-integration copy. If consent is No, show a gentle opt-in state and exclude cycle from Patterns.
Patterns (a strongest screen): cards with clean mini charts, each with "Why am I seeing this?" revealing the underlying data. Wording "HerCode noticed a possible pattern." Never causation. Example insights: "You tend to complete phone calls more often before 12:00." / "You reported low capacity on 5 of the last 6 days after two consecutive social evenings." / "Your average energy has been higher around this part of your cycle during your last three cycles." / "You usually finish household tasks more consistently when they are broken into steps under 10 minutes."
Partner / BroCode setup: optional connection, toggles per tier, signal picker, live preview panel.
BroCode preview: styled as a distinct companion app. "Mia has shared: Low capacity today." Things you can take over (decide dinner, start laundry, pick up groceries), derived from Shared tasks and Decision-load rules. Helpful today: "Try solving one practical thing without asking her to manage the process." Includes "This is what your partner can currently see." and a "Manage sharing" button. Never exposes precise cycle info, health info or personal notes unless she explicitly chooses.
Decision load: Dinner > Partner can decide / Weekend plans > Ask me first / Groceries > Partner can handle / Cleaning > Partner can take over / Purchases over 1000 NOK > Ask first / Visitors > Always ask.
Daily review: "How did today feel?" capacity 1-5; "What helped?" chips (smaller tasks, quiet time, partner help, exercise, clear plan, extra sleep, less social activity); "What made things harder?" chips; Skip. Feeds Patterns.
Notifications (concept): Gentle / Normal / Only important. iOS-style banner previews and a "Simulate" button (no real push): "Dentist in 45 minutes. You usually need around 20 minutes to get there." / "You have 25 free minutes before school pickup. Want one small task?" / "You've moved this phone call three times. Make it a 2-minute first step?" / "You marked today as bare minimum. I've hidden the non-essential reminders." Show which reminders bare-minimum mode suppressed.
Onboarding (max 5 steps, skippable): (1) What do you want help with (multi-select: remembering things, starting tasks, overwhelm, routines, planning, household life, appointments, emotional load, cycle patterns, relationship coordination). (2) What usually happens when there's too much (I freeze / I jump between things / I forget / I avoid everything / I become exhausted / depends on the day). (3) Cycle permission. (4) Partner connection choice. (5) "What should we call you?" A "Use demo (Mia)" shortcut is on step 1. The name entered replaces "Mia" everywhere; demo data always loads.

DEMO PERSONA
Mia: adult woman with ADHD, partner Jonas, one child (Ellie), household responsibilities, variable energy, voluntarily tracks her cycle (about 3 cycles of manual data, currently day 21). Today, fixed: 09:30 Dentist, 12:00 Lunch, 15:30 School pickup, 17:00 Grocery delivery. Priority tasks: Call insurance, Buy shampoo, Laundry. Essentials: Eat lunch, Take medication, School pickup. Brain dump: rain boots for Ellie, gift for Emma, book hair appointment, try taco recipe, return jacket, look at green lamp, remember school photo Wednesday. Many completed and moved tasks across 90 days. No lorem ipsum.

SAFETY
Not medical treatment, diagnostic software, a replacement for ADHD treatment, or a predictor of hormonal behavior. Present cycle and behavior data as personal observations and correlations. Privacy is a visible product feature.

ACCEPTANCE TEST (2-minute demo path)
Onboarding skip > check-in > Today (complete a task) > "I'm overwhelmed" > Bare minimum (plan regenerates) > Brain dump (watch categorization, edit one) > "I can't start" on Clean kitchen > Patterns ("Why am I seeing this?") > change a sharing toggle > BroCode preview updates > Decision load edit > Daily review. All state persists across reload; Reset demo data restores the seed.
