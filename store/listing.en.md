## Description (both stores, ≤4000)
BRO CODE is a coach for guys who want to be a better partner — built around her cycle, written in plain bro, backed by actual studies.

Enter one date (the first day of her last period). That's the only required field. From there the app knows where she is in her cycle and gives you exactly three things a day:

• DAILY REP — one specific, small thing to do for her today, tuned to her phase and her profile. Tap DONE when you've actually done it. +15 points, real-life points only.
• POP QUIZ — one question a day from Bro University. 64 questions, every one traced to real research. Right answer: +10 and it explains why. Wrong answer: +3, and it still explains why.
• HER WEATHER — a meteorologist's take on her week. "Severe sensitivity advisory. Chance of the wrong tone landing wrong: 82%. Recommended gear: steady presence, no surprises." With a 7-day outlook.

THE GAME YOU WIN IN REAL LIFE
Six ranks from Rookie to Hall of Fame Bro. 28 trophies — Bid Catcher, The Repairman, Iron Chef, Human Being — every one earned out there, not in here. A streak that survives real life: you bank a Bro Pass every seven days, so one rough day doesn't zero a 90-day run. No points for scrolling.

BUILT FOR HER, SPECIFICALLY HER
Ten seconds of profile turns generic cycle advice into advice about the actual person you live with: anxiety, ADHD, what comforts her, her pace, her social battery, her cravings, her contraception (it changes how the calendar reads). Two differently-configured partners get zero overlapping tips.

RIGHT NOW
She's crying. She's angry. She's gone quiet. You messed up. You're spiraling. Tap the situation, get phase-aware moves and exact phrases.

NOT VIBES. SOURCES.
Heat therapy rivals painkillers for cramps (25-trial meta-analysis, 2025). Partner support measurably lowers premenstrual symptom severity (PLOS ONE, 2025). She needs 18mg of iron a day; you need 8. And an honesty section on the things the internet oversells — "cycle syncing" workouts, the five love languages, the famous 90% divorce-prediction stat. When the science and her actual words disagree, believe her.

THE ARCHIVE
495 entries — every tip, study, quiz question, decoder phrase and health deep-dive — searchable by a word.

WHAT IT ISN'T
Not a doctor. Not a tracker for her (it's a coach for you, and it works best when she knows it exists). Not data collection: no account, no server, no analytics. Everything lives on your phone. Export or wipe whenever.

Free. No ads. Works offline.
